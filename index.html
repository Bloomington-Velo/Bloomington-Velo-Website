01 — The club

Who we are

Bloomington Velo is a performance-oriented cycling club with an emphasis on racing and training, riding out of Bloomington, Indiana on the limestone-country roads south of town.

We also mentor and develop riders of varied backgrounds and abilities — showing new members the roads, the etiquette, and the fitness to ride safely at pace.

Race-minded, but nobody gets dropped alone.

04 — Joining

Join us

There is no sign-up form and no membership fee. Just turn up to a ride: come to a Tuesday or Thursday ride at 5:45 PM at the Bryan Park pool parking lot, say hello, and tell us what you ride.

Once you have ridden with us, ask about joining the club GroupMe — that is where we post pace changes and weekend plans.

Questions first? Email bloomingtonvelocycling@gmail.com or find us on Instagram.

No invitation needed, just a bike and a Tuesday at 5:45.
