01 — The club

Who we are

Bloomington Velo is a performance-oriented cycling club with an emphasis on racing and training. We ride out of Bloomington, Indiana, on the limestone-country roads that make this one of the best places to ride in the state — long climbs, empty pavement, and a town full of people who take it seriously.

We also mentor. The roster runs to 34 members from a wide range of backgrounds and abilities, and the club keeps a library of 51 documented routes so nobody has to guess where the good roads are. If you can hold a wheel and you want to get faster, there is a group here for you.

Race-minded, but nobody gets dropped alone.

04 — Getting started

How to join a ride

No sign-up, no waiting list. Turn up, or say hello first.

  • 1Come to a Tuesday or Thursday ride — 5:45 PM at the Bryan Park pool parking lot.
  • 2Say hello. Tell us what you ride and we will point you at the right group.
  • 3Join the club GroupMe so you hear about pace changes and weekend plans.
Email the club Instagram

Come ride with us

Tuesdays and Thursdays at 5:45 PM from Bryan Park. Saturdays and Sundays from the Sample Gates. Bring a spare tube.
