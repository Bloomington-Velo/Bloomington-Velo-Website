Three homepage directions

Same club, same words, same facts — three different ways of presenting them. Every mockup carries identical content (header and nav, hero, who we are, next rides, the Strava widget slot, a join call-to-action, footer) so the comparison is about design, not copy. Pick one and the rest of the site gets built on it.

What is identical in all three

  • Nav: Team, Ride, Calendar, Routes, Sponsors, Contact
  • The same headline, the same "who we are" paragraphs, the same three sample rides
  • Tue & Thu 5:45 PM from Bryan Park; weekends 40–100+ mi at 18–20 mph from the Sample Gates
  • 34 members, 51 routes, Strava club 329602, @bloomingtonvelo

About the missing photographs

  • No stock photos and no external images — everything is drawn in CSS or inline SVG
  • A: an abstract contour field, the hills south of town
  • B: a chevron "paceline" taken from the club seal, with halftone and grain
  • C: deliberately none, which is the whole argument for C
  • Each stand-in is labelled on the page so nobody mistakes it for a finished hero

Worth weighing before you choose

  • B is the only one that gets noticeably worse if real club photography never arrives
  • C is the only one that gets no better if it does
  • A sits in the middle: good with the placeholder, better with a photo
  • Check each on your phone, not just here — the previews are scaled
  • Nothing here ships. Whichever wins becomes assets/css/site.css.
