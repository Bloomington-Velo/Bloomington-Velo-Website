Who we are

Race-minded. Nobody gets dropped alone.

Bloomington Velo is a performance-oriented cycling club with an emphasis on racing and training. We ride out of Bloomington, Indiana, on the limestone-country roads that make this one of the best places to ride in the state.

We also mentor. The roster runs to 34 members from a wide range of backgrounds and abilities, and the club keeps a library of 51 documented routes so nobody has to guess where the good roads are. If you can hold a wheel and you want to get faster, there is a group here for you.
